# NASA Space Apps 2026: "Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data"

Challenge page: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/adapting-farms-with-nasa-data/
**Submission deadline: Sunday, November 15, 2026, 11:59 pm local time.**
Demo format: either a 7-slide presentation (title and intro slides included) or a 30-second video. All materials in English.

## The brief, condensed
Farmers face shifting temperatures, changing rainfall, water shortages, extreme weather and declining soil health, which makes crop-rotation choices hard. The task is to build a **decision-support tool** that combines **NASA Earth observations** with local soil information, crop characteristics and farmer priorities, so farmers can explore rotation strategies that strengthen soil health and adapt to changing conditions.

The brief suggests:
- **Compare at least three rotation strategies, including the current one.** For each, explain why it is suggested, the supporting evidence, the benefits, the information gaps, and the risks that need local review.
- **Highlight timing and opportunities:** when fields are bare, seasons when crops are vulnerable, and rotations that interrupt pest cycles, reduce water demand, improve infiltration or add income.
- **Harmonize the data.** AI and statistical methods, combining multi-resolution data (SMAP, ECOSTRESS, GPM IMERG, Landsat/MODIS/VIIRS, GEDI, soils) onto a common grid. Explain uncertainty and where local expertise must validate.
- **Map it.** Maps of erosion risk, water-stress hotspots and frost-prone areas, plus timelines and scenario sliders.
- **Design for farmers.** Usable without technical training. Farmers enter their own priorities and constraints. The tool supports the farmer's judgment and does not replace it.

## Judging (each criterion scored 1-5, equal weight)
- **Impact:** a significant problem, wide reach, the potential to change the world.
- **Creativity:** novel, or a meaningful improvement on existing tools.
- **Validity:** scientifically sound, feasible, usable.
- **Relevance:** directly addresses the challenge, with **NASA data integral rather than decorative**.
- **Presentation:** clear, compelling storytelling.

The team's top priority is **IMPACT that judges can internalize in seconds.**
